# Post-freeze amendment policy

No final artifact freeze exists yet. After an actual approved freeze, record amendment date, author, reason, allowed scope, old/new artifact hashes, affected claims and real approval. Preserve original package/tag. Publication/accessibility or approved packaging fixes are distinguishable from new scientific experiments; never silently substitute post-freeze results. Final commit SHA belongs in an external receipt or annotated tag after commit, avoiding self-referential manifest edits.
